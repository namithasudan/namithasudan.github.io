Namitha Sudan is a doctoral researcher in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Tirupati, India, where she works on the environmental and economic assessment of pavement systems with the MAPSmart research cluster — (Novel and Green) Materials for sustainability, Asset management, and Performance monitoring of Smart infrastructure. She holds a Bachelor of Technology in Civil Engineering from TKM College of Engineering, Kollam, affiliated to the A P J Abdul Kalam Technological University.

Her work asks what a road actually costs across the whole life cycle of a pavement system, including the use stage and end-of-life. She is particularly interested in evaluating the environmental performance of pavement systems through life-cycle assessment and life-cycle optimization, integrating structural and functional pavement performance with environmental impacts. Her interests also include pervious concrete pavement and its potential for sustainable stormwater management, along with the development and application of geopolymer and other alternative cementitious materials that reduce the environmental footprint of infrastructure. Taken together, the work aims at performance-based, environmentally informed approaches to designing more sustainable and resilient transportation infrastructure.
